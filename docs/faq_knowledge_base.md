# Ethiopia Innovation Hub - Platform Knowledge Base & FAQ

## 1. General Questions

### Q: What is the Ethiopia Innovation Hub?
The Ethiopia Innovation Hub is a dedicated national platform where Ethiopian universities, enterprises, tech hubs, NGOs, and government agencies host and participate in hackathons and innovation challenges. It unifies event discovery, team formation, project submission, eligibility screening, multi-round judging, and local prize disbursement in a single system optimized for Ethiopia's languages, institutions, and payment infrastructure.

### Q: Who is the platform for?
The platform serves six primary roles:
- **Participants**: Students, early-career developers, and innovators building projects, finding teammates, and creating a public portfolio.
- **Organizers**: Universities, companies, and agencies configuring and managing challenges end-to-end.
- **Sponsors**: Companies funding themed challenge tracks within larger hackathons.
- **Judges**: Faculty members, technical leaders, and industry experts scoring submissions against structured rubrics.
- **Mentors**: Advisors providing guidance to teams during hackathons.
- **Platform Admins**: Operators managing institution verifications, content moderation, and platform health.

### Q: How is the Ethiopia Innovation Hub different from generic global platforms (e.g., Devpost) or using Google Forms & Telegram?
Unlike global tools or informal channels:
1. **Bilingual by Design**: Native support for **English** and **Amharic (አማርኛ)**.
2. **Local Payment Payouts**: Automated disbursement via **Telebirr**, **CBE Birr**, and **Chapa**.
3. **Institutional Verification**: Email domain verification (`.edu.et`, company domains) providing verified badges for trust.
4. **Low-Bandwidth & SMS Resiliency**: Low-data UI modes with SMS notifications as a fallback channel for low-connectivity regions.
5. **Unified Discovery**: One searchable national catalog instead of fragmented Telegram groups and Google Forms.

### Q: Is the platform free to use?
Yes. Creating an account, browsing events, building a team, submitting projects, and accessing the public showcase is **100% free** for participants. Organizers and sponsors can host events under standard platform institutional plans.

### Q: How does the platform comply with Ethiopia’s Data Protection Proclamation?
All user data and submission content are handled in strict compliance with Ethiopian data residency and privacy regulations. Personal data is never sold to third-party marketers or advertisers, and data access is strictly role-scoped.

## 2. Platform Basics

### Q: Which devices can I use to access the platform?
You can access the platform on any device with a web browser — including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. The responsive frontend adapts automatically to small screens and low-bandwidth connections.

### Q: How do I switch between English and Amharic?
Click the language selector (🌐 **EN / አማ**) in the top navigation bar. Switching language instantly updates the navigation, buttons, form prompts, and notification preferences.

### Q: Who can contact me on the platform?
- Registered participants can message you if you are in the same team or if you enable open requests in the **Teammate Finder**.
- Event organizers and assigned mentors can reach out to your team regarding hackathon logistics.
- Your personal phone number is **never publicly visible** unless you explicitly share it.

## 3. Hackathons, Challenge Tracks & Teams

### Q: What is the difference between a main Hackathon and a Sponsor Challenge Track?
- **Main Hackathon**: The overall event hosted by an anchor organizer with a primary theme, general prize pool, and overall judging rubric.
- **Sponsor Challenge Track**: A sub-category inside the event funded by a specific sponsor (e.g., *"Best FinTech App using Telebirr API"*). Teams can submit their project to the overall event AND opt into one or more sponsor tracks for additional prizes.

### Q: Can I participate as an individual, or do I need a team?
It depends on the rules set by the event organizer. Some hackathons allow solo entries, but most require teams of 2 to 5 members. If you don't have a team, register as an **Individual Looking for Teammates** to use the **Teammate Finder**.

### Q: How does the Teammate Finder work?
1. Open the hackathon dashboard and select **Teammate Finder**.
2. Filter participants by required skills (e.g., *Frontend*, *Machine Learning*, *UI/UX*, *Pitching*).
3. Send an invite link or join request directly through the platform.

### Q: Can I change team members after submitting our project?
- **Before the Registration/Submission Deadline**: The Team Leader can add members, remove members, or transfer leadership.
- **After the Deadline**: Roster changes are **locked** to preserve judging integrity. Any exceptions require organizer approval.

## 4. Usernames & Institutional Identity

### Q: What are platform Usernames and handle links?
When setting up your profile, you can pick a unique username (e.g., `@selam_dev`). You receive a shareable personal handle link (`hub.et/@selam_dev`) that opens your public portfolio and verified credentials.

### Q: How do Institutional Verification Badges work?
When registering with an accredited email address (e.g., `name@aau.edu.et` or `developer@company.et`), the system automatically verifies your domain and grants a **Verified Institutional Badge** (🎓 University / 🏢 Enterprise) on your profile and team entries.

### Q: What if someone is impersonating me or my organization?
Impersonation violates platform policies. Report fake profiles or unauthorized organizational accounts by clicking **Report Account** or emailing `abuse@innovationhub.et`.

## 5. Project Submissions & Public Showcase

### Q: What deliverables must be included in a project submission?
Standard submission requirements include:
- **Project Title & Tagline**
- **Detailed Solution Description** (Problem statement, architecture, tech stack)
- **Public Code Repository Link** (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket)
- **Video Demo Link** (2-3 minute YouTube, Vimeo, or Loom video)
- **Optional Pitch Deck** (PDF format, up to 10MB)

### Q: Can I edit my submission before judging begins?
Yes. You can edit your project draft as many times as needed until the countdown timer reaches zero. Once the submission phase closes, the project status changes to `Locked for Screening`.

### Q: What time zone is used for submission deadlines?
All deadlines are explicitly enforced in **East Africa Time (EAT / UTC+3)**. A real-time countdown clock is displayed on all event pages.

### Q: What is the Public Showcase?
After winners are announced, eligible projects enter the **Public Showcase**. This indexed directory highlights Ethiopian innovation, allowing university leaders, investors, and corporate recruiters to discover top talent and solutions.

## 6. Organizers, Screening & Judging

### Q: How do organizers set up eligibility screening and rubrics?
In the **Organizer Dashboard**:
- **Eligibility Screening**: Filter out non-compliant entries (e.g., broken links, off-topic projects) before assigning them to judges.
- **Rubric Configuration**: Define criteria (e.g., *Innovation*, *Impact*, *Technical Execution*, *Presentation*) and assign relative weights (e.g., 1–5 scale).

### Q: How does multi-round and blind judging work?
Organizers can configure multi-round judging (e.g., Preliminary Screening → Technical Review → Finalist Pitch). Blind judging hides team member names and institutional affiliations from judges to ensure unbiased scoring.

### Q: Can organizers export analytics and participant reports?
Yes. Organizers can export anonymized or full event reports (CSV/PDF) showing registration velocity, skill distribution, submission completion rates, and judge score breakdowns.

## 7. Prizes & Local Payments (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Chapa)

### Q: How are cash prizes disbursed to winning teams?
Cash prizes are paid out directly through integrated local Ethiopian payment rails:
- **Telebirr**
- **CBE Birr**
- **Chapa (Direct Bank Transfer / Card)**

Upon winner confirmation, the Team Leader enters the verified payout details, and payouts are disbursed with automated transaction logging.

### Q: What documentation is required to claim a cash prize?
To prevent fraud and satisfy audit compliance:
- Verified identity of the Team Leader / recipient.
- Active Telebirr/CBE Birr mobile number or commercial bank account matching the account name.
- Signed **Prize Split Agreement** among team members for team prizes.

## 8. Security, Privacy & User Safety

### Q: How secure is my data on the platform?
The platform utilizes industry-standard security protocols:
- TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit.
- AES-256 encryption for data at rest.
- Password hashing using PBKDF2/Argon2.
- JWT-based authorization tokens for API routes.

### Q: How does the 24/7 AI Support Assistant handle my data?
The AI Assistant runs on a private, scope-aware retrieval-augmented generation engine (`apps/support_ai`).
- It only searches **public knowledge base articles, published hackathon rules, and official FAQs**.
- It **never shares private messages, draft submissions, or unverified personal data** with external AI services.

### Q: Can I enable 2-Step Verification (2FA)?
Yes. You can protect your account by enabling 2-Step Verification in **Settings > Privacy & Security**, requiring an SMS or Authenticator App code alongside your password.

## 9. Your Account & Lifecycle

### Q: How do I change my primary phone number or email address?
Go to **Account Settings > Profile Details**, enter your new mobile number or email, and verify it via the confirmation code sent to the new address/phone.

### Q: How does account inactivity self-destruction work?
To conserve resources and uphold privacy, accounts that remain completely inactive for **18 consecutive months** are scheduled for automatic self-destruction. All cloud data associated with inactive accounts will be permanently erased after notice.

### Q: What is a Frozen / Restricted Account?
If an account is flagged for spam, plagiarism, code theft, or harassment, platform administrators may freeze the account. While frozen, users can read content but cannot post or submit projects. Appeals can be submitted via `appeals@innovationhub.et`.

## 10. Troubleshooting & Support

### Q: I did not receive my login or registration code via SMS/Email. What should I do?
1. Check your **Spam/Junk** email folder.
2. Confirm your mobile number includes the Ethiopian prefix `+251` (e.g., `+251911XXXXXX`).
3. Click **Resend Code via SMS** or switch to email verification.
4. If using a corporate or university email, verify that emails from `no-reply@innovationhub.et` are permitted by your network admin.

### Q: Why can't I edit my project submission?
Editing is disabled if:
- The submission countdown timer has expired.
- The project status has moved to `Under Judging` or `Archived`.
- You are logged in as a team member rather than the designated **Team Leader**.

### Q: How do I contact platform support?
- **AI Support Assistant**: Click the chat widget on the bottom right for instant answers.
- **Support Ticket**: If the AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, click **Submit Support Ticket** to reach our volunteer and administrative support team.

## 11. System Architecture & Core Stack

### Q: What is the architectural design of the Ethiopia Innovation Hub backend?
The system is built as a **Modular Monolith** using **Python 3.12, Django 5, and Django REST Framework (DRF)**.
- It is partitioned into 12 domain-driven applications (`accounts`, `organizations`, `hackathons`, `registrations`, `teams`, `submissions`, `judging`, `showcase`, `notifications`, `analytics`, `platform_admin`, `support`, `support_ai`).
- Each app enforces a strict four-layer architecture:
  `models.py (Data Domain) ➔ services.py (Business Logic) ➔ serializers.py (Validation) ➔ views.py (API Layer)`
- All primary keys utilize **UUIDv4** (`models.UUIDField`) to prevent enumeration attacks and facilitate distributed record creation.

### Q: How is contract synchronization maintained between the backend and frontend?
The API contract is formally defined in **OpenAPI 3.0** format (`contracts/openapi.yaml`).
- The contract is auto-generated directly from Django REST Framework serializers and views using `drf-spectacular`.
- Whenever an endpoint, serializer, or model schema changes, developer tooling runs `./scripts/export_contract.sh` to update `contracts/openapi.yaml`.
- The frontend team consumes this file to generate TypeScript client types via `npm run sync-contract`, eliminating API drift.

### Q: Why a Modular Monolith over microservices?
A modular monolith provides strict boundary separation between domains while eliminating distributed transaction complexity, network latency, and operational overhead during initial deployment phases. Domains communicate through explicit Service-layer interfaces (`services.py`).

## 12. Support AI & Vector Retrieval Engine (`apps/support_ai`)

### Q: How does vector retrieval work in `apps/support_ai`?
Knowledge base articles, FAQs, hackathon descriptions, rules, and rubric criteria are broken into text chunks and indexed as `DocumentChunk` instances in PostgreSQL.
- Each `DocumentChunk` contains:
  - `source_type` (`article`, `faq`, `hackathon_rules`, `hackathon_rubric_criteria`, `showcase_project`)
  - `source_id` (UUID of the source record)
  - `visibility` (`public`, `organizer_only`, `platform_admin_only`)
  - `embedding` (Dense vector embedding)
  - `metadata` (JSON payload containing tags, category, and chunk index)

### Q: How does the system prevent unauthorized data disclosure in RAG queries?
Data leaks are prevented at the database layer via **`ScopedChunkManager`**.
- Calling `.objects.all()` or `.objects.filter()` directly on `DocumentChunk` for retrieval is **strictly prohibited**.
- The sole sanctioned retrieval path is `retriever.retrieve_chunks()`, which enforces `ScopedChunkManager.visible_to(user=user, hackathon_id=..., organization_id=...)`.
- If a user is an anonymous participant, `_allowed_visibility_levels()` restricts chunk matching strictly to `public` visibility. Organizer-only rubrics or internal admin logs are structurally filtered out before cosine similarity search is performed.

### Q: How are AI chat sessions and latency logged?
Every user interaction with the AI assistant creates a `ChatSession` record. Each response logs a `ChatMessage` storing:
- `retrieved_chunk_ids` (List of UUIDs of chunks used for context)
- `llm_model` (Model identifier used for generation)
- `latency_ms` (End-to-end execution latency in milliseconds)
- User feedback (`AIFeedback` rating 1–5 and optional comment) for RAG accuracy tracking.

## 13. Payment Rail Integration & Cryptography

### Q: How are local Ethiopian payment gateways (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Chapa) integrated?
The platform uses an abstract **Payment Provider Service Interface** (`apps/support/services.py` / `apps/organizations`).
- **Webhooks**: Incoming payment notifications from Telebirr, CBE Birr, or Chapa require cryptographic signature verification using HMAC-SHA256 with pre-shared API secret keys.
- **Payload Verification**: Webhook handlers verify transaction IDs, currency codes (`ETB`), amount matching, and timestamp freshness before transitioning payout states.

### Q: How is payout idempotency enforced during bulk prize disbursements?
Bulk payout operations to winning teams utilize idempotent transaction keys (`payout_uuid`).
- Before sending a transaction request to a local payment gateway, a `PaymentTransaction` record is created in state `PENDING` with a unique idempotency key.
- If a gateway request times out or requires retries, subsequent retries send the identical idempotency key, preventing double-disbursement of funds.

## 14. Security, Authentication & Data Protection

### Q: What authentication mechanisms are used?
Authentication is powered by **JSON Web Tokens (JWT)** via `djangorestframework-simplejwt`.
- **Access Tokens**: Short-lived (15-minute expiration) signed RS256/HS256 tokens sent via `Authorization: Bearer <token>` headers.
- **Refresh Tokens**: Rotating long-lived refresh tokens stored securely in HTTP-only cookies or encrypted local storage.
- Custom user model (`AUTH_USER_MODEL = "accounts.Account"`) handles multi-role permissions.

### Q: How are file uploads (code repositories, pitch decks, demo media) secured?
- File uploads are validated for MIME type, file extension, and maximum byte size (e.g., 10MB for PDFs).
- Uploaded files are stored using Django's abstracted storage layer (`STORAGES["default"]`), saving files with randomized UUID filenames to prevent path traversal (`../../`) attacks.
- Direct execution of uploaded scripts is disabled at the web server layer (`Nginx` / storage configuration).

## 15. Scalability, Storage & Low-Bandwidth Optimizations

### Q: How is the system optimized for low-bandwidth networks in Ethiopia?
1. **JSON Payload Compression**: Gzip/Brotli compression enabled on all DRF API responses.
2. **Field Truncation**: Serializers support sparse fieldsets (`?fields=id,title,status`) to minimize transfer overhead.
3. **SMS Fallback Notification Pipeline**: Critical alerts (verification codes, team invites, deadline warnings) automatically fall back to low-payload SMS via local gateway aggregators if websocket/push delivery fails.

### Q: What is the caching and background processing architecture?
- **Caching & Celery**: The application is architected for **Celery + Redis** task execution (configured via `config/settings/base.py` and `docker-compose.celery-example.yml`).
- Heavy tasks — such as vector embedding generation, PDF analytics report compilation, and mass email/SMS delivery — are offloaded to asynchronous worker queues.

## 16. Mitigation Against Technical Vulnerabilities & Attacks

### Q: How does the system defend against SQL Injection, XSS, and CSRF?
- **SQL Injection**: All database interactions use Django's ORM parameterized queries. Raw SQL queries are forbidden.
- **XSS (Cross-Site Scripting)**: User-generated content (project descriptions, markdown articles) is sanitized using `bleach` to strip dangerous HTML tags (`<script>`, `onload`, `javascript:`).
- **CSRF**: API endpoints using cookie-based session auth enforce standard Django `CSRFToken` verification.

## 17. AI Vector Search & RAG Chunking Guidelines

### Q: How does `apps/support_ai` protect against Prompt Injection and RAG Data Poisoning?
- **Prompt Injection**: System prompts strictly isolate retrieved context (`DocumentChunk.text`) from instructions using clear demarcation tags (`<context>...</context>`). User inputs are sanitized to strip system instruction overrides.
- **RAG Poisoning**: Only published articles and verified hackathon rules created by authenticated, role-verified Organizers or Admins can be indexed into vector store. Drafts and unverified user submissions are excluded.
